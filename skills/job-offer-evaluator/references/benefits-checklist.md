# Benefits checklist

Use when the offer is vague or the job is outside Spain. Mark each item as stated / statutory default / unknown.

- Base pay, payment structure (12/14 payments, hourly), currency and FX risk
- Bonus, equity/RSUs, vesting, signing bonus, review cycle
- Health insurance (who pays, family coverage), private vs public system
- Retirement: statutory contributions, employer match, pension plan
- Paid time off (days, public holidays), unpaid for B2B
- Sick leave (paid? from which day? covered for contractors?)
- Parental leave and its pay level
- Equipment, home-office stipend, coworking, training budget, conferences
- Notice period, non-compete, IP clause, exclusivity (important for B2B)
- Relocation or visa support (abroad), tax residency effects, social security regime (A1 certificate, double taxation)
- Contract type specifics: B2B means autónomo cuota, IRPF quarterly payments, VAT handling (reverse charge for non-EU clients), gestor costs
